Privacy Policy — Visual AI Agent

Last updated: September 19, 2026 · Applies to version 1.1.0+

Summary

Visual AI Agent (VAIA) is a local-first browser telemetry and screen-analysis tool. Your browsing data, screenshots, and AI analyses are stored on your own computer and sent only to a receiver server you choose (by default, your own machine at http://localhost:8787). The extension's publisher operates no servers, receives no data, and has no access to anything the extension records.

What the extension collects

  • Activity events: page views, clicks, scrolls, keystroke metadata (that typing occurred), navigation and error events.
  • Screenshots: periodic captures of the visible tab, only when capture is enabled.
  • AI analyses: descriptions of captured screens produced by the vision provider you configure.

Where the data goes

  • Locally: IndexedDB inside your browser profile, and (optionally) the local receiver's SQLite database plus screenshot files.
  • Your receiver: event, screenshot, insight, and session records go to the endpoint you configure. Use VAIA_TOKEN to require a shared token on every request when your receiver is reachable beyond localhost.
  • Your vision provider: if you enable AI analysis, captured screenshots are sent to the provider you choose (NVIDIA NIM, OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, or a local Ollama). That provider processes the images under its own privacy policy. With the built-in mock provider, nothing leaves your machine.

The extension's publisher never receives any of this data.

What is protected by design

  • Password, credit-card, and similar sensitive fields are detected and their values are masked before storage.
  • Screenshots are dropped by default on pages detected as sensitive.
  • Host denylist/allowlist lets you exclude or restrict which sites are monitored.
  • dataRetentionDays auto-prunes stored data.
  • Every sync channel (events, screenshots, insights) can be switched off.

Chrome Web Store permission disclosures

  • tabs, windows — read active-tab URL/title to record page views and attention time; list and focus tabs on request.
  • scripting + host_permissions: <all_urls> — inject the activity recorder and DOM-analysis helpers into the pages you browse, on any site, so tracking and analysis work everywhere you enable them.
  • webNavigation — detect page loads and navigations for session and attention accounting.
  • alarms — schedule periodic captures, flushes, and scheduled tasks.
  • notifications — surface agent notifications you request.
  • downloads — save files when you invoke the download ability.
  • storage, unlimitedStorage — store your settings and the local offline archive.
  • offscreen documents — re-encode screenshots without visible UI.

Data retention and deletion

Data lives only in the locations listed above until you delete it: uninstall the extension, clear the extension's storage, delete the receiver's data/ directory (or your VAIA_DATA_DIR), or set dataRetentionDays to prune automatically.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be reflected in the extension's changelog and on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Open an issue at the project repository for any privacy question or data request.
